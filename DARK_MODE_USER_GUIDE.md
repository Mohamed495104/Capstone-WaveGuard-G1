# Dark Mode User Guide

## How to Use Dark Mode in WaveGuard

WaveGuard now supports dark mode, allowing you to choose between a light and dark theme for a more comfortable viewing experience.

## Accessing Dark Mode

### On Desktop (Laptop/Computer)

1. **Navigate to Profile Settings**
   - Click on your profile icon in the top-right corner of the navigation bar
   - You will be directed to your profile page

2. **Open Settings Tab**
   - In your profile page, you'll see two tabs: "Profile" and "Settings"
   - Click on the "Settings" tab

3. **Enable Dark Mode**
   - Scroll down to the "Appearance" section
   - You'll see a "Dark Mode" toggle switch with a moon/sun icon
   - Click the toggle switch to enable dark mode
   - The theme will change immediately

### On Mobile (Phone/Tablet)

1. **Open Profile Menu**
   - Look for the profile icon in the top-right corner of the screen
   - Tap on the profile icon to open the menu

2. **Enable Dark Mode**
   - In the dropdown menu, you'll see three options:
     - Profile
     - Dark Mode (with toggle switch)
     - Logout
   - Tap the toggle switch next to "Dark Mode"
   - The theme will change immediately

## Features

### Automatic Persistence
- Your theme preference is automatically saved
- The app will remember your choice even after you close your browser
- When you return to WaveGuard, your preferred theme will be active

### Smooth Transitions
- Theme changes happen smoothly with gentle transitions
- All colors, backgrounds, and text update seamlessly

### What Changes in Dark Mode?

When you enable dark mode, the following elements adapt:

1. **Backgrounds**
   - Main background changes from white to dark slate
   - Cards and panels use darker shades

2. **Text**
   - Text becomes lighter for better readability
   - All text maintains proper contrast for easy reading

3. **Navigation**
   - Header and navigation bar adapt to dark theme
   - Active items remain clearly visible
   - Hover effects are optimized for dark backgrounds

4. **Forms and Inputs**
   - Input fields have darker backgrounds
   - Borders and placeholders are adjusted for visibility

5. **Footer**
   - Footer adapts to dark theme
   - Contact cards and links remain clearly visible

## Benefits of Dark Mode

### Eye Comfort
- Reduces eye strain in low-light environments
- Less harsh light emission, especially at night

### Battery Saving
- On OLED and AMOLED screens, dark mode can save battery
- Dark pixels require less power

### Personal Preference
- Many users prefer dark interfaces
- Better for different lighting conditions

### Professional Look
- Modern, sleek appearance
- Aligns with contemporary design trends

## Tips

1. **Try Both Modes**: Test both light and dark modes to see which you prefer in different settings
2. **Lighting Conditions**: Use light mode in bright environments and dark mode in dim lighting
3. **Time of Day**: Consider switching to dark mode in the evening to reduce eye strain
4. **Personal Comfort**: Choose the mode that feels most comfortable for your eyes

## Switching Back to Light Mode

You can switch back to light mode at any time by:

1. **Desktop**: Go to Profile > Settings > Appearance and toggle off Dark Mode
2. **Mobile**: Open the profile menu and toggle off Dark Mode

Your preference will be saved automatically.

## Troubleshooting

### Theme Not Saving
- Make sure your browser allows localStorage
- Check that cookies are enabled
- Try clearing your browser cache and setting the preference again

### Colors Look Wrong
- Refresh the page (F5 or Ctrl+R)
- Try toggling dark mode off and on again
- Clear your browser cache

### Toggle Not Working
- Make sure JavaScript is enabled in your browser
- Try refreshing the page
- If the issue persists, contact support

## Accessibility

Dark mode in WaveGuard is designed with accessibility in mind:

- All text maintains WCAG AA contrast ratios
- Colors are carefully chosen for readability
- Icon meanings remain clear in both modes
- No essential information is color-dependent
